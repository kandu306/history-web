Nha Mac — Lap Trieu & Thinh Tri

Chapter 1/2 · Nhuong danh giu thuc co the la nuoc co khon ngoan — neu ban biet ro dau la danh, dau la thuc

Tom tat chapter

Mac Dang Dung — ngu dan khong dong doi — leo len dinh quyen luc bang cach luon dung ve phia "hop phap": dep loan cho trieu dinh, tich luy quyen luc tung buoc trong 20 nam. Con trai Mac Dang Doanh mang lai giai doan thinh tri thuc su (1529–1540) ma ngay ca su doi thu cung phai thua nhan. Nam 1541, doi mat gong kim Minh (Bac) + Le Trung Hung (Nam), Mac Dang Dung chon quy lay Nam Quan de giu trieu dai — doi danh du lay 50 nam ton tai them.

Phe thang viet su Nhuong danh giu thuc

Boi canh mo dau

Nha Hau Le (Le So) sau 30 nam lien tiep vua kem — Uy Muc, Tuong Duc, roi cac vua bu nhin do quyen than dung len. Thang Long loan lac: quyen than giet nhau, Tran Cao noi day xung de, vua bo chay. Trong boi canh do, ai dep duoc loan thi nguoi do co quyen.

◈

Dien bien chinh

1483–1527 · Mac Dang Dung len ngoi

Sinh 1483, gia dinh ngu dan, khong dong doi quy toc. 1508 thi do vo cu. 1521 dep tan Tran Cao, duoc phong Thai su. 1527 ep Le Cung Hoang nhuong ngoi sau 5 nam thuc quyen. Tai sao khong ai ngan duoc? (1) Luon dung ve phia "hop phap" — dep loan cho trieu dinh; (2) khong con tuong gioi nao khac sau 30 nam vua kem; (3) dan chi can ai dep duoc loan.

1527 · Le Cung Hoang thoai vi

Vua khoang 18–20 tuoi, ngoi ngai 5 nam nhung chua bao gio co quyen that. Mac dung hinh thuc "chieu nhuong ngoi hop phap" — mo hinh hoc tu Ho Quy Ly ep Tran Thieu De. Le Cung Hoang bi giet khong lau sau khi thoai vi.

1529–1540 · Thang Long thinh tri duoi Mac Dang Doanh

22 khoa thi trong 65 nam ton tai, dung chu ky 3 nam/lan — khong gian doan ke ca thoi chien. Kinh te, ngoai thuong soi dong qua cang Van Don. Thue nhe, hinh phat khoan hoa. Ngay ca Dai Viet su ky toan thu (do phe Le-Trinh bien soan) cung phai ghi nhan. Giai doan nay bi lich su "xoa" vi phe thang cuoc can Nha Mac la phan dien.

1533 · Nguyen Kim lap Le Trung Hung

Nguyen Kim — tuong Le cu — chay sang Ai Lao, tim duoc Le Duy Ninh, ton lam Le Trang Tong, lap Le Trung Hung. Tu Ai Lao dan dua quan ve Thanh Hoa (1543) — can cu dia Le Loi tung dung. Mo dau chien tranh Nam-Bac trieu keo dai 60 nam.

1541 · Cu quy lay Nam Quan

Mac Dang Dung (58 tuoi, Thai thuong hoang) doi mat gong kim: Minh phia Bac + Le Trung Hung phia Nam ngay cang manh. Chon quy lay, coi ao hoang bao, dang dat bien gioi doi lay cong nhan. Thuc chat: Minh khong dong quan, khong can thiep noi bo — Mac doi danh lay thuc, giu trieu dai ton tai them 50 nam.

Tai sao xay ra nhu vay?

Phe thang cuoc viet su

Nha Mac bi lich su danh gia thap vi phe thang cuoc viet su — Chua Trinh can tinh chinh danh bang cach to ve Mac la "nguy trieu".

Diem yeu that su

Khong co vung can cu dia — kiem soat dong bang Bac Bo giau co nhung de bi tan cong tu Thanh Hoa. Mat Thang Long la mat tat ca.

Bai hoc cot loi ung dung thuc te

Bai hoc 01

Nhuong danh giu thuc

Biet phan biet "nhuong bo chien thuat" (tam lui de tien sau) voi "dau hang chien luoc" (tu bo muc tieu cot loi) la ky nang lanh dao hiem.

Bai hoc 02

Leo len bang con duong hop phap

Mac Dang Dung khong dao chinh — ong dep loan, duoc phong tuoc, tich luy 20 nam. Khi ai cung thay ong nam quyen that thi da qua muon de ngan.

Ap dung thuc te

Khi dam phan/thuong luong bat loi — tu hoi: dieu minh sap nhuong bo la danh (the dien, hinh thuc) hay thuc (gia tri cot loi)? Nhuong danh giu thuc co the la nuoc co khon ngoan.

He qua & Dan sang Chapter 2

Ngay lap tuc

Nha Mac kiem soat dong bang Bac Bo giau co; Le Trung Hung hinh thanh o Thanh Hoa-Nghe An tu 1533.

Dai han

22 khoa thi dao tao nhieu nhan tai — ke ca cho doi thu. Nen ngoai thuong (Hoi An sau nay thua huong).

Dan den Chapter 2

Hai chinh quyen song song, khong ben nao du manh tieu diet ben kia. Sau khi Mac Dang Doanh mat (1540), cac doi vua Mac ke tiep kem hon → can can bat dau nghieng ve phia Le-Trinh.
